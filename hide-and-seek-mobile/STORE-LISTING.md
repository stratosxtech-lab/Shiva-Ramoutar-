## Full description

Hide & Seek turns any park, campus, office or home into a live game board.

Everyone plays on their own phone. Hiders get a head start. Seekers follow a warm zone
that shrinks as the clock runs down, listen for chirps, and tag hiders by scanning the
QR code on their screen. Scores, a podium and a leaderboard settle who's best.

GAME MODES
• Classic: one seeker hunts everyone else.
• Infection: every hider who's found joins the seekers.
• Shadow Teams: two teams take turns hiding and seeking.
• Decoy Duel: hiders drop decoys, seekers fight back with a reveal pulse.
• Indoor: no GPS needed. Find hiders by their chirps.

BUILT FOR GROUPS AND TEAMS
• Group passes for parties, camps and clubs of up to 20 players.
• Corporate plans for team-building, onboarding and offsites, with a dashboard for
  organisers and an engagement report for leadership.

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Location is shared only during a game and is never shown to other players.
• Geofenced play zones warn players before they wander off.

Free to play Classic with up to 6 players. Upgrade for every mode and bigger groups.
